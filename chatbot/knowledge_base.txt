# ReservApp - Knowledge Base

## What is ReservApp?
ReservApp is a room reservation management system that allows members to browse available rooms (salles) and make reservations for specific dates and timeslots.

## How to book a room (membres)
1. Log in to your account
2. Click on "Salles" in the navigation bar
3. Browse the available rooms
4. Click on a room to see its details and available timeslots
5. Choose a date and an available timeslot
6. Click "Réserver" to confirm your reservation
7. You will receive a confirmation email with a QR code

## Rooms (Salles)
- Each room has a name, description, and capacity
- Rooms have available timeslots (créneaux) for booking
- Timeslots already booked by others are shown as unavailable
- You can view all rooms without being logged in

## Reservations
- Members can view their reservations under "Mes réservations"
- Each reservation has a status: active or past
- A confirmation email with a QR code is sent after booking
- You cannot book a timeslot that is already taken
- You cannot book timeslots in the past

## Account & Profile
- Members can update their profile information
- Members can upload a profile photo
- Members can change their password
- Login can be done with email/password or Google account

## Reviews (Avis)
- Members can leave a star rating (1 to 5 stars) and a comment about the app
- Reviews are visible to everyone on the Avis page
- Members can leave multiple reviews

## Calendar
- The calendar page shows all reservations visually
- Members can see reservations by day, week, or month

## Security
- The app uses OTP (one-time password) verification for admin accounts
- If you log in from a new device or location, you will receive a security alert email

## FAQ
Q: I forgot my password, what do I do?
A: Click on "Mot de passe oublié?" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Q: Can I cancel a reservation?
A: Contact the administrator to cancel a reservation.

Q: How do I change my profile photo?
A: Go to your profile page by clicking your name in the navbar, then upload a new photo.

Q: What happens after I book a room?
A: You receive a confirmation email with a QR code that serves as your reservation proof.

Q: Can I book multiple rooms?
A: Yes, you can make multiple reservations for different rooms and timeslots.

Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes, ReservApp uses secure authentication including Google OAuth and email verification.
